⚡ Vitality: Your Physical Foundation

Your body is your most important business asset. Vitality encompasses your physical health, energy management, and recovery practices that fuel everything else you do.

1. How consistently do you maintain healthy sleep patterns?
2. How would you rate your current physical fitness and activity level?
3. How intentional are you about nutrition and fueling your body?
4. Do you build recovery and renewal into your work rhythms?

🎯 Execution: Your Ability to Get Things Done

Execution is about focus, systems, and the discipline to translate plans into results. It's the bridge between strategy and success.

5. How effectively do you maintain focus on your highest-priority work?
6. How strong are your systems and processes for getting work done?
7. How well do you manage distractions and protect your attention?
8. How consistently do you follow through on commitments and complete important projects?

🛡️ Resilience: Your Capacity for Challenge

Resilience is your ability to handle stress, adapt to change, bounce back from setbacks, and maintain emotional equilibrium under pressure.

9. How effectively do you manage stress and regulate your emotions?
10. How quickly do you recover and learn from setbacks or failures?
11. How well do you maintain perspective and optimism during difficult times?
12. How adaptable are you when circumstances change unexpectedly?

🤝 Connection: Your Relationships & Support

No entrepreneur succeeds alone. Connection encompasses your relationships, network, team, and the support systems that amplify your capabilities.

13. How strong is your professional network and support system?
14. How effectively do you delegate and leverage others' capabilities?
15. How would you rate the quality of your personal relationships outside of work?
16. How actively do you invest in learning from others and seeking feedback?

🌟 Vision: Your Clarity & Direction

Vision provides the "why" that drives sustained effort. It's your clarity of purpose, alignment with values, and long-term strategic thinking.

17. How clear are you on your long-term vision and goals?
18. How well aligned is your work with your core values?
19. How regularly do you engage in strategic thinking and planning?
20. How meaningful and purposeful does your work feel to you?
