Frequently Asked Questions

Who do you work with?
We work with ambitious professionals at all career stages—from recent graduates seeking direction to senior executives preparing for C-suite roles. Whether you're in tech, finance, healthcare, or any other industry, our frameworks can be adapted to your specific situation.
How long does a typical engagement last?
Engagement length varies based on your goals. Career clarity sessions typically take 4-6 weeks, transition planning 8-12 weeks, and ongoing leadership development 3-6 months. We customize the timeline to your needs and pace.
What's included in your services?
Our services include personalized assessments, one-on-one coaching sessions, strategic planning tools, resume and LinkedIn optimization, interview preparation, accountability check-ins, and customized resources. You get comprehensive support tailored to your specific career goals.
Do you provide ongoing support?
Yes! We offer ongoing coaching retainers, monthly check-ins, and on-demand support for professionals who want continuous guidance as they advance in their careers. Many clients stay with us for years as trusted advisors.
What makes your approach different?
We combine Fortune 500 corporate insight with entrepreneurial mindset. You get strategic frameworks from the corporate world paired with the agility and creativity of startup culture. We focus on practical, actionable strategies—not just theory—and commit to your success with hands-on support.
